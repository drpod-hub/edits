# Invincible vs. Conquest Rematch — YouTube Short

Vertical (1080x1920, ~71s) edit of Prime Video's *"Invincible vs. Conquest Rematch"* clip
(Invincible Season 4). The source is 640x360, so it is upscaled with lanczos plus light sharpening.

## What the edit does
- **Cold-open hook:** opens on the blood-spray climax with "ROUND 2 GOT BRUTAL 😳", white flash,
  then "RUN IT BACK ⏪" into Mark's "You're never breathing again."
- **Persistent title:** "CONQUEST WANTED A REMATCH... 💀".
- **Cut from 5:12 to the planet fight:** the space subplot and the Prime Video end card are gone.
  The "Where's Mark?" Nolan/Oliver cutaway stays as a comedy beat ("DAD OF THE YEAR 🏆" →
  "MEANWHILE, MARK: 🩸").
- **Meme stickers** on each impact (Air Mail ✈️, Mark Got Yeeted 🚀, Free Landscaping 🪨,
  Breathing: Optional 😵, Dental Plan: Cancelled 🦷, Why Is He Smiling?! 😳...), ending on a
  comment prompt: "WHO WINS ROUND 3? 👇".
- Captions for the few spoken lines, zoom punches, bass hits on the big impacts, progress bar,
  1.1x pacing, loudness normalised to -14 LUFS.

## Rebuild
```
python3 build.py SOURCE.mp4 invincible_conquest_short.mp4
```
Rendering is in `../shortkit.py`; everything clip-specific is data at the top of `build.py`.
